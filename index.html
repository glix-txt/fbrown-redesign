01 — What we do

Three trades.
One tidy finish.

  1. 01

    Suspended ceilings

    Grid and tile ceiling systems hung from the structure above, leaving space for services, lighting and access while giving a clean, level finish below.

  2. 02

    Linings

    Board linings to walls and ceilings, ready for decoration. A straight, flat surface over existing structure, with the detailing around openings done carefully.

  3. 03

    Metal stud partitions

    Lightweight steel stud walls to divide and reshape space: framed, boarded and set out to suit the layout, from single offices to whole floors.

02 — About us

Specialist fit-out, from a Stirling office.

We concentrate on the parts of a fit-out people notice most: the ceilings overhead and the walls around them.

F Brown (Scotland) Ltd carries out interior fit-out work across Scotland, covering suspended ceilings, linings and metal stud partitions. Keeping to a focused set of trades means the people on site know the systems well and the work is done to a consistent standard.

We hold CHAS and Constructionline accreditation, so main contractors and clients can check our health and safety and compliance credentials before work starts.
